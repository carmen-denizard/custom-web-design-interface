Delicate Adornments

Welcome to Crystal Flowers, where simplicity meets meaning in every handcrafted piece. Each item is thoughtfully designed and made using ethically sourced, conflict-free materials from environmentally responsible suppliers — because beauty should never come at a cost to people or the planet! No two pieces are exactly alike, as our designs are inspired by nature’s own artistry, reflecting the uniqueness of both the person who wears it and the hands that shape them. At Crystal Flowers, you’ll find quiet elegance with a conscience.

The Latest

Every time we put together a new piece or collection, we team up with artists and other creative folks to design something that’s not only beautiful, but also full of personality. We’re all about turning creative ideas into pieces you can actually wear and enjoy every day.
